As a career guidance expert, here's how I'd map out your options after 12th with Maths — organized by the kind of future you might want, not just a random list.

Step 1: Know your broad options
A. Engineering & Technology (most popular path)

B.Tech/B.E. in Computer Science, AI/ML, Mechanical, Civil, Electronics, etc.
Entry via JEE Main/Advanced, state CETs, or private exams (BITSAT, VITEEE)
Good if you enjoy problem-solving, building things, or coding
B. Pure Sciences & Research

B.Sc. in Maths, Statistics, Physics, or integrated M.Sc. at IISER/CMI/ISI
Leads to research, teaching, or higher studies (M.Sc./PhD)
Good if you love theory and want to go deep into a subject
C. Computer Applications & Data

BCA, B.Sc. Data Science/Statistics
No entrance exam usually needed; fast-growing field
Good if you like coding/analytics but want a lighter, quicker path than engineering
D. Commerce, Finance & Economics

B.Com, BBA, Economics Honours, Actuarial Science, CA/CFA/CS
Good if you're numbers-oriented but more interested in business, markets, or money than machines
