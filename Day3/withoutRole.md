Great choice of stream — Maths after 12th opens doors across engineering, technology, finance, defence, research, and more. Here's a breakdown:

1. Engineering & Technology
The most common path for Maths students.

B.Tech/B.E. — Computer Science, Mechanical, Civil, Electrical, Electronics, AI/ML, Data Science, etc. (via JEE Main/Advanced, state CETs, or private entrance exams)
B.Arch (Architecture) — needs Maths + an aptitude test (like NATA or JEE Paralysis 2)
2. Pure & Applied Sciences
B.Sc. in Maths, Statistics, Physics, Computer Science, or combinations like Maths + Computer Science
Leads to careers in research, data analysis, teaching, or higher studies (M.Sc., PhD)
3. Computer Applications & Data
BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications) — great for software development, no entrance exam usually needed
B.Sc. Data Science / Statistics — booming field with careers in data analytics, machine learning, and AI
4. Commerce, Finance & Economics
Maths gives you an edge here even without a Commerce background.

B.Com / BBA — business and finance careers
Economics (B.A./B.Sc. Honours) — strong quantitative programs at places like DSE, ISI
Chartered Accountancy (CA), CFA, Company Secretary (CS) — professional courses, open to any stream
Actuarial Science — highly maths-intensive, leads to high-paying careers in insurance/risk analysis
5. Defence & Government Services
NDA (National Defence Academy) — direct entry after 12th for Army, Navy, Air Force
Merchant Navy — via entrance exams like IMU-CET
6. Design & Architecture
B.Des (Design) — via UCEED, NID entrance exams
B.Planning — urban and regional planning
7. Research-Oriented Paths
Integrated M.Sc. at IISERs, IITs, CMI, ISI — for those interested in core Maths, Physics, or interdisciplinary research
8. Aviation
Commercial Pilot Training — Maths + Physics required, plus a separate pilot training program
Common entrance exams to know:
JEE Main/Advanced — engineering (IITs, NITs)
BITSAT — BITS Pilani
NDA — defence
CUET — for many central universities (B.Sc., B.Com, BA programs)
UCEED/NID DAT — design
