Section 1: Explanation

What is Prompt Engineering?

Prompt engineering is simply the skill of asking AI tools the right way. Think of it like giving directions to a very capable but very literal assistant — if you're vague, you get a vague answer. If you're clear and specific, you get exactly what you need.

Why it matters when using AI tools like Claude

Claude (and any AI model) doesn't read your mind — it responds to the words you give it. The same question can produce a shallow, generic answer or a sharp, useful one depending entirely on how it's phrased. Learning to write better prompts means you get more value out of every single interaction, without needing any technical or coding skills.

Section 2: Weak vs Improved Prompt Example

Weak prompt:

"Write about marketing."

This gives Claude almost nothing to work with — no audience, no goal, no format. The output will likely be generic and unfocused.

Improved prompt:

"Act as a marketing consultant. Write a 150-word LinkedIn post explaining 3 practical digital marketing tips for small business owners just starting out. Use a friendly, encouraging tone and end with a question to spark engagement."

This version tells Claude the role, the audience, the length, the tone, and the goal — so the output arrives ready to use.
